Chainstay Sensor Clip

A3144 hall sensor · 19 mm stay · PETG

A snap-on C-clip holds the heat-shrunk sensor in a channel, with 1.2 mm of plastic between its printed face and the spoke magnet. The cable leaves out the back and is zip-tied along the stay.

Print

  • PETG or ABS. PLA works but gets brittle and creeps in the sun.
  • Stand it up: the C shape flat on the bed, 14 mm tall. Every layer is a full C, so it flexes without splitting.
  • 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill. No supports; the end stop bridges 6.6 mm.

Fit the sensor

  1. Solder the cable to the A3144 legs, heat-shrink each joint, then one sleeve over the whole thing.
  2. Slide it in from the back, printed face toward the wheel, until it stops.
  3. Snap the clip on so the channel points at the magnet path. Set a 3 to 5 mm gap.
  4. A drop of CA glue or a zip-tie through the stay keeps it from rotating.

The A3144 only switches for the magnet's south pole facing its printed side. No pulses in the app? Flip the magnet.
